# Frontier Project UFRGS

> Exploring the frontiers of science, technology and innovation.

## About
The Frontier Project is an innovation initiative based at the Institute of Physics of the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS).

Our mission is to connect researchers, students, companies, startups and public institutions to transform scientific knowledge into real-world impact.

We believe that the greatest innovations emerge at the intersection of disciplines. By combining physics, engineering, artificial intelligence, computation, data science and entrepreneurship, we create an environment where ambitious ideas can become transformative technologies.

---

## Vision
To become a national reference in deep-tech innovation, fostering a culture where scientific excellence drives technological development and societal impact.
